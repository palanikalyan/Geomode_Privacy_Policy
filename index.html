GeoMode

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

GeoMode is an Android application that automatically changes your device's sound profile based on geographical locations (hotspots) configured by you.

This Privacy Policy explains how GeoMode accesses, uses, and stores information when you use the application.

2. Location Information

GeoMode requires location access to detect when your device enters or exits hotspots that you have configured.

Location information is used on your device to support this functionality. GeoMode is designed to process hotspot and location information locally and does not transmit it to a developer-operated server.

Background location access may be required for hotspot detection when the application is not actively open.

3. Information Storage

GeoMode may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Hotspot names
  • Hotspot geographical coordinates
  • Hotspot radius or boundary settings
  • Sound profile preferences associated with hotspots
  • Application settings

This information is used only to provide the application's location-based automation features. GeoMode does not provide cloud synchronization or user accounts.

4. Data Sharing

GeoMode does not sell your personal information. The application does not transmit hotspot or location information to a developer-operated server.

Android, Google Play services, or other platform components may process information as necessary to provide device and location services, subject to their own privacy policies.

5. Permissions

Depending on your device and application version, GeoMode may request:

  • Location permission: To detect entry into and exit from saved hotspots.
  • Background location permission: To support hotspot detection when the app is not open.
  • Notification policy access: To change sound or Do Not Disturb settings when required.
  • Notification permission: To display relevant application notifications where applicable.

You can manage these permissions through your Android device settings. Disabling required permissions may affect some application features.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Hotspot configurations and preferences remain stored locally until you delete them or clear the application's data. Uninstalling GeoMode generally removes its locally stored application data.

7. Children's Privacy

GeoMode is not specifically directed at children. The application does not knowingly collect personal information through a developer-operated server.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or privacy practices change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
